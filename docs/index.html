Our 5-Point Manifesto

  • 1. Cooling-Off Periods for Elites

    No retiring judges or top bureaucrats can accept political appointments or corporate board seats immediately after retirement. A mandatory 5-year cooling-off period must be implemented.

  • 2. The Anti-Defection Enforcement

    Elected officials who switch political parties after winning an election will face an automatic 20-year ban from participating in any electoral process.

  • 3. 50% Representation for Women

    Mandatory 50% reservation for women in all cabinet positions and major legislative decision-making bodies to ensure true democratic parity.

  • 4. Transparent Funding

    The party and all state institutions will operate strictly under the Right to Information Act. Zero anonymous donations. If you fund us, your name goes on a public ledger.

  • 5. Educational Justice

    Immediate systemic reforms and accountability for national examination boards. Strict penalties for those involved in exam paper leaks to protect the future of students.
